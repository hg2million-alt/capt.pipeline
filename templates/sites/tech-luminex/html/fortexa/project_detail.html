Retail Enterprise Threat Monitoring

Project Overview

A leading retail enterprise operating across multiple regions faced a surge in attempted cyber intrusions targeting customer data and point-of-sale systems. With millions of daily transactions, the organization required real-time monitoring and advanced detection to safeguard sensitive information.

Project Overview
  • Increasing phishing and malware attacks targeting employees and retail systems.
  • Vulnerabilities within legacy infrastructure that created compliance risks.
  • Limited visibility into real-time threats across multiple retail branches.
  • High-value customer data, making the business a prime target for cybercriminals.
Our Solution

Fortexa deployed a tailored Threat Monitoring Framework, integrating:

  • AI-Powered Threat Detection to identify anomalies before they escalate.
  • 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC) for continuous monitoring.
  • Network Segmentation & Endpoint Protection to isolate and secure vulnerable systems.
  • Compliance Mapping ensuring adherence to PCI-DSS and global retail security standards.
